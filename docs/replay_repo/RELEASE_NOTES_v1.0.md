Replay remote and death notebook for League of Legends. Download `Replay-v1.0-windows.zip` below, unzip, run `Replay.exe`.
Windows may warn that the file is unsigned: **More info → Run anyway.**

### What it does

- **A remote for League's replay player.** Play/pause, ±10 s, 0.5×–4× and **Follow me**, which keeps the camera on your champion — also after every jump. Downloads and opens the replay through the League client with one click.
- **The whole game on one timeline.** Your deaths, missed chances and bookmarks, every kill and objective. Click any moment and the replay jumps there.
- **Every death, with context.** Who killed you and with how many helpers, solo or not, the enemy jungler, gold against your lane opponent, your level, and whether the enemy took an objective right after.
- **Tag why you died.** Positioning, no vision, overextended, bad trade, avoidable gank, mechanics, forced by team or worth it — and whether you could have avoided it. **N** jumps to the next untagged death. Saved as you click.
- **Missed chances and bookmarks.** **M** and **B** mark the replay's current moment.
- **Insights.** Why you die and what you miss across every game you've tagged, per champion, the share you called avoidable, and the one thing to focus on next session.
- **Matchup notes.** Rate the lane and write what to do next time. **Draft** shows your notes against that champion in champ select.

### Good to know

- Needs the League client for downloading and watching replays. Replays only work on the patch they were played on.
- The first **Watch** turns on League's local replay API (`EnableReplayApi=1` in `game.cfg`; a backup is kept).
- Shares games with **Review**, so anything Review has fetched is already here. Notes stay on your PC.
- Keys: **Space** play/pause · **← →** 10 s · **N** next untagged · **M** missed chance · **B** bookmark · **F** follow me.
