Ranked account analyzer for League of Legends. Download `Review-v1.0-windows.zip` below, unzip, run `Review.exe`.
Windows may warn that the file is unsigned: **More info → Run anyway.**

### What it does

- **Performance radar.** Eight skills — fighting, farming, vision, aggression, survival, teamplay, objectives, consistency — each scored 0–100 against players in your role, with a 90 % range. Click a skill for what it's made of, how it changed, your best and worst games and one thing to work on.
- **Compare with any rank.** Pick Gold, Plat, Emerald, Diamond or Master+ and every score and chart is measured against real games at that rank in your region, so a 54 means the same thing on every account. *Both scales* shows your own lobbies and the rank you picked side by side.
- **Strengths and weaknesses** in plain words, each with a concrete tip. Only shown when the difference is clear in at least 20 games.
- **Trends.** Rolling averages per stat with the rank's average as a dashed line, and a verdict (improving / declining / stable) only when the change is bigger than the noise.
- **Champions.** Win rate, KDA, CS, gold vs your opponent, damage share and score per champion; ◆ marks your best picks.
- **Wins vs losses.** The stats that separate your wins from your losses, ranked.
- **Lane phase.** Gold, CS and XP against your direct opponent, minute by minute, in wins and losses.
- **Deaths.** Every death on the minimap by game phase, how many hand the enemy an objective, and when in the game they happen.
- **Tilt and sessions.** Your win rate by game number in a session and after one, two and three losses in a row — and by weekday and hour.
- **Every game.** Open any match for its gold curve, deaths on the map and that game's radar.
- **Compare with a friend.** Put another Riot ID on the radar next to yours.

### Good to know

- Data comes from Riot's official API. Games are cached on your PC, so only new games are downloaded next time.
- Solo/Duo, Flex or both. EUW, EUNE, NA, KR and the other regions.
- Right-click any chart to save it as a PNG.
